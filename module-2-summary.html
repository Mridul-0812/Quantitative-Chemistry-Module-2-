Module 2 Formula Summary

The Chemist's Toolkit: Every Equation and Data Set required for Year 11 NSW Chemistry

1. Core Stoichiometry & Mass Formulas

Relative Atomic Mass ($A_r$)

$$A_r = \sum \left( \frac{\text{Isotopic Mass} \times \% \text{ Abundance}}{100} \right)$$
  • Weighted average mass of naturally occurring isotopes relative to Carbon-12

The Mole (Mass)

$$n = \frac{m}{MM}$$
  • $n$: number of moles (mol)
  • $m$: mass (g)
  • $MM$: molar mass (g/mol)

The Mole (Particles)

$$n = \frac{N}{N_A}$$
  • $N$: number of particles (atoms, molecules, ions)
  • $N_A$: Avogadro's constant ($6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ mol}^{-1}$)

Percentage Composition

$$\% \text{ by mass} = \frac{m_{\text{element}}}{m_{\text{compound}}} \times 100$$
  • $m_{\text{element}}$: mass of specific element in 1 mol of compound
  • $m_{\text{compound}}$: molar mass of whole compound

Percentage Yield

$$\% \text{ Yield} = \frac{\text{Actual Yield}}{\text{Theoretical Yield}} \times 100$$

Both yields must be measured in identical units (mass or mol).

Density ($\rho$)

$$\rho = \frac{m}{V}$$
  • $\rho$: density ($\text{g/mL}$ or $\text{g/L}$)
  • $m$: mass (g)
  • $V$: volume ($\text{mL}$ or $\text{L}$)

2. Concentration & Solution Formulas

Molarity / Concentration

$$c = \frac{n}{V}$$
  • $c$: concentration ($\text{mol/L}$ or M)
  • $n$: amount of solute (mol)
  • $V$: volume of solution (L)

Dilution Formula

$$c_1V_1 = c_2V_2$$
  • $c_1, V_1$: Initial (Stock) concentration and volume
  • $c_2, V_2$: Final (Diluted) concentration and volume

Mass Concentration

$$c_{\text{g/L}} = \frac{m}{V}$$
  • $m$: mass of solute (g)
  • $V$: volume of solution (L)
  • Conversion: $c_{\text{g/L}} = c_{\text{mol/L}} \times MM$

Percentage by Mass (% w/w)

$$\% \text{ w/w} = \frac{m_{\text{solute}}}{m_{\text{solution}}} \times 100$$

Mass of solute per 100g of total solution.

Percentage by Volume (% v/v)

$$\% \text{ v/v} = \frac{V_{\text{solute}}}{V_{\text{solution}}} \times 100$$

Volume of solute per 100mL of total solution.

Mass/Volume Percentage (% w/v)

$$\% \text{ w/v} = \frac{m_{\text{solute}} (\text{g})}{V_{\text{solution}} (\text{mL})} \times 100$$

Mass of solute in grams per 100mL of total solution.

Parts Per Million (ppm)

$$\text{ppm} = \frac{m_{\text{solute}} (\text{mg})}{m_{\text{solution}} (\text{kg})} = \frac{m_{\text{solute}} (\text{mg})}{V_{\text{solution}} (\text{L})}$$

Used for trace amounts of solute in aqueous solutions.

3. Gas Laws & Gas Stoichiometry Formulas

Molar Gas Volume ($V_M$)

$$n = \frac{V}{V_M}$$
  • STP ($0^\circ\text{C}, 100\text{ kPa}$): $V_M = 22.71 \text{ L/mol}$
  • SLC ($25^\circ\text{C}, 100\text{ kPa}$): $V_M = 24.79 \text{ L/mol}$

Boyle's Law

$$P_1V_1 = P_2V_2$$

At constant temperature ($T$) and mole count ($n$).

Charles' Law

$$\frac{V_1}{T_1} = \frac{V_2}{T_2}$$

At constant pressure ($P$) and mole count ($n$). Temperature must be in Kelvin ($K = ^\circ\text{C} + 273.15$).

Gay-Lussac's (Amontons') Law

$$\frac{P_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2}{T_2}$$

At constant volume ($V$) and mole count ($n$). Temperature must be in Kelvin.

Avogadro's Gas Law

$$\frac{V_1}{n_1} = \frac{V_2}{n_2}$$

At constant pressure ($P$) and temperature ($T$). Equal volumes of gases contain equal numbers of moles.

Combined Gas Law

$$\frac{P_1V_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2V_2}{T_2}$$

Used when mole count ($n$) is fixed. Temperature in Kelvin.

Ideal Gas Law

$$PV = nRT$$
  • $P$: Pressure ($\text{kPa}$)
  • $V$: Volume ($\text{L}$)
  • $n$: Amount of gas ($\text{mol}$)
  • $R$: Universal Gas Constant ($8.314 \text{ J K}^{-1} \text{mol}^{-1}$)
  • $T$: Absolute Temperature ($\text{K}$)

Gay-Lussac's Law of Combining Volumes

$$V \propto n$$

When gases react at constant $T$ and $P$, reaction volumes are proportional to their stoichiometric coefficients.

6. Solubility Rules (NAGSAG)

Rule / Ion Solubility Exceptions
Nitrates ($\text{NO}_3^-$) Always Soluble None
Acetates ($\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^-$ / $\text{C}_2\text{H}_3\text{O}_2^-$) Always Soluble None
Group 1 Ions ($\text{Li}^+, \text{Na}^+, \text{K}^+ \dots$) Always Soluble None
Sulfates ($\text{SO}_4^{2-}$) Soluble $\text{Pb}^{2+}, \text{Ba}^{2+}, \text{Sr}^{2+}, \text{Ca}^{2+}, \text{Ag}^+, \text{Hg}_2^{2+}$
Ammonium ($\text{NH}_4^+$) Always Soluble None
Group 17 (Halides: $\text{Cl}^-, \text{Br}^-, \text{I}^-$) Soluble $\text{Ag}^+, \text{Pb}^{2+}, \text{Hg}_2^{2+}$
Carbonates ($\text{CO}_3^{2-}$) / Phosphates ($\text{PO}_4^{3-}$) Insoluble Group 1 ions and $\text{NH}_4^+$
Hydroxides ($\text{OH}^-$) Insoluble Group 1 ions, $\text{NH}_4^+$, $\text{Ba}^{2+}$, $\text{Sr}^{2+}$, $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ (slightly soluble)
