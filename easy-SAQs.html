Question 1: The Mole & Molar Mass [4 Marks]

Consider a 15.0 g sample of Calcium Carbonate ($CaCO_3$).

a) Calculate the molar mass of $CaCO_3$. [1 Mark]

b) Determine the number of moles in the 15.0 g sample. [1 Mark]

c) Calculate the total number of Oxygen atoms in this sample. [2 Marks]

Hint: Use $n = \frac{m}{MM}$. For part (c), remember there are 3 moles of Oxygen atoms for every 1 mole of $CaCO_3$.

Marking Criteria

MarksDescription
4Correct molar mass, correct moles (3 sig figs), and correct total atoms using Avogadro's constant.
2-3Minor calculation error in one part; correct process followed.

Worked Solution

a) $MM = 40.08 + 12.01 + (3 \times 16.00) = 100.09 \text{ g/mol}$

b) $n = \frac{15.0}{100.09} = 0.1498... \approx 0.150 \text{ mol}$

c) $n(O) = 3 \times 0.1498 = 0.449 \text{ mol}$.
Atoms $= 0.449 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} = 2.71 \times 10^{23} \text{ atoms}$.

Question 2: Boyle's Law & Graphing [5 Marks]

A gas occupies 2.0 L at 100 kPa. If temperature is constant, plot the following data points (P, V): (100, 2.0), (200, 1.0), (400, 0.5).

a) Draw the curve connecting these points. [2 Marks]

b) State the relationship between Pressure and Volume shown. [1 Mark]

c) Predict the volume at 800 kPa. [2 Marks]

Worked Solution

a)

b) Pressure is inversely proportional to Volume ($P \propto \frac{1}{V}$).

c) $P_1V_1 = P_2V_2 \rightarrow (100)(2.0) = (800)(V_2) \rightarrow V_2 = 0.25 \text{ L}$.

Question 3: Percentage Composition [3 Marks]

Calculate the percentage by mass of Nitrogen in Ammonium Nitrate ($NH_4NO_3$).

$MM(NH_4NO_3) = (2 \times 14.01) + (4 \times 1.008) + (3 \times 16.00) = 80.05 \text{ g/mol}$

$\%N = \frac{28.02}{80.05} \times 100 = 35.0\%$

Question 4: Molarity [3 Marks]

A solution is made by dissolving 5.85 g of $NaCl$ in 250 mL of water. Calculate the molarity.

$n = \frac{5.85}{58.44} = 0.1 \text{ mol}$. $V = 0.25 \text{ L}$. $C = \frac{0.1}{0.25} = 0.4 \text{ M}$.

Question 5: Dilution [2 Marks]

How much water must be added to 100 mL of 1.0 M $HCl$ to make it 0.1 M?

$C_1V_1 = C_2V_2 \rightarrow (1.0)(100) = (0.1)(V_2) \rightarrow V_2 = 1000 \text{ mL}$.

Water added $= 1000 - 100 = 900 \text{ mL}$.
